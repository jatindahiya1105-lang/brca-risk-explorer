Values reflect aggregate SHAP contributions across the full cohort from the trained genes‑only XGBoost model — they are not computed live from any individual's expression data (the trained model and per‑patient SHAP values weren't exported from the pipeline for this demo). Directional consistency is the ratio of mean signed SHAP to mean |SHAP|: a low value means the gene's effect on predicted risk depends heavily on interactions with other genes rather than a simple "higher expression → higher risk" rule — expected behavior for a tree‑based model with 5,000 features.
